# 🧪 Concept: Advanced Packaging (先进封装)

**Brief Summary**:
High-density semiconductor packaging techniques (such as CoWoS, TGV, and 3D stacking) used to bridge the memory and computing gaps when Moore's Law scaling hits physical boundaries.
