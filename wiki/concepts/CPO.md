# 🧪 Concept: Co-Packaged Optics (共封装光学)

**Brief Summary**:
Next-generation optical packaging architecture that places optical transceivers directly on the same multi-chip module as the ASIC/switch, bypassing traditional pluggable copper/fiber bottlenecks.
